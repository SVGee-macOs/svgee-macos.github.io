Whether you're managing a massive icon library or tweaking assets for your next project, SVGee makes working with Scalable Vector Graphics effortless and intuitive.

Blazing-Fast Preview

Instantly render SVGs at any scale without lag. Zoom, pan, and inspect paths with precision—perfect for reviewing icons and illustrations in seconds.

Intelligent Color Detection

Detect and extract colors from any SVG with one click. Generate palettes, copy hex values, or apply changes across multiple files efficiently.

Seamless Conversion

Convert SVGs to PNG, PDF, or other formats in bulk. Retain quality and fidelity while optimizing for web, print, or app assets.
